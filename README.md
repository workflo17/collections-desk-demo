# Collections Desk showcase

Collections Desk runs the receivables half of a bookkeeper's job for a business of one to ten people. It puts the terms in writing, issues the invoice, writes reminders in the owner's words, notices a Zelle or Venmo payment from the bank's email, and drafts a demand letter only when the owner approves. It never sends a message on its own.

This repository holds only the built showcase: https://workflo17.github.io/collections-desk-demo/

The showcase is a recorded copy of the desk running Harbor Studio, an invented photography studio. Every page and PDF is the desk's own output from the day it was built. The guided tour replays real button presses: confirming a Zelle payment, writing and approving a reminder, drafting a demand letter, a refused invoice, and the client's side of signing and paying. Every name, address and amount is invented, and nothing here is legal advice.
