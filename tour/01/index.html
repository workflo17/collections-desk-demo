How the desk works

  1. Terms in writing first

    The desk writes the agreement from the job, checks it against New York's freelance law, and gives the client a page to sign. No invoice goes out without one.

  2. The invoice

    Issued on the signed agreement at the amounts it allows, as a PDF and a page the client can pay from.

  3. Reminders in your words

    Before the due date, on it, then three, seven and fourteen days late. Each one waits for your approval, and a reply from the client stops them until you have read it.

  4. The payment, noticed

    Zelle, Venmo, PayPal and Cash App emails are read for the amount and the invoice number. You confirm once you see the money in your app.

  5. A letter, if it comes to that

    The late interest your agreement allows, a demand letter that cites the law, then a small-claims packet. You approve, send and file each one yourself.

What it will not do

  • Send anything for you. You read every reminder and letter and send it from your own address.
  • Count money because an email says it arrived. Fake payment emails are a common scam.
  • Invent a fee. Late interest comes only from the agreement you both signed, under the state's cap.
